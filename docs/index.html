Seven complementary modalities

DeepCatch fuses seven orthogonal cfDNA signal sources through a self-supervised Transformer foundation model:

Fragmentomics

5-mer end motifs + fragment size distribution + DELFI/WPS coverage profiles. Highest-signal modality on tumor-naive detection.

Methylation

CpG-island-region-aware β-value features with depth normalization. Genome-wide methylation panel for tissue-of-origin.

Mutation panel

Per-locus Poisson LLR aggregation across tumor-suppressor panel. Best at MRD-style ultra-low VAF (≤0.1%).

Tissue deconvolution

cfSort-style decomposition into contributing cell types. Estimates tumor fraction & tissue-of-origin.

CNV profile

5Mb + 100kb median-normalized coverage ratio across the genome. Median-normalization is the unlock for low-depth data.

Nucleosome positioning

WPS — windowed protection scores around TSS. Captures chromatin accessibility changes in tumor.

Integrated-gradients explanations

Per-prediction attribution for every modality. Required for clinical interpretability, not just AUC.
